Greetings from Youth Empowering India.

We are delighted to invite the First-Year Engineering students of ARAVLI COLLEGE OF ENGINEERING AND MANAGEMENT to a free career guidance webinar being organised under the Youth Empowering India Campaign.

Participation: Free

Key Highlights
Career guidance and insights from industry professionals

Understanding career opportunities and pathways in engineering

Information about upcoming skill-building workshops and initiatives

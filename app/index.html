Notice Decoder

Paste a college notice. Get clear events, catch clashes, add them to your calendar.

The rules run in your browser. Only when they are unsure, the AI check sends the notice text (nothing else) to Claude. You are offline; everything except the AI check and the class calendar still works.

1. Paste a notice

Press Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) to decode. On a phone, install the app and share any notice to it straight from WhatsApp.

Photos, PDFs and Word files are read on this device (nothing is uploaded). The first photo downloads the reader once (about 10 MB); after that it works offline. Drop a file on the box, or paste a screenshot.

          5. Add to calendar and share

          Class representatives: share once, the whole class adds it in one tap. The events travel inside the link; nothing is stored on a server.

          6. Class calendar

          Class representatives publish once; the class subscribes once to a calendar that updates itself when a notice changes. You see exactly what will change before anyone else does.

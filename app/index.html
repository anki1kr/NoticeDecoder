Notice Decoder

Paste a college notice. Get clear events, catch clashes, add them to your calendar.

Runs in your browser. Your notice never leaves this device. You are offline; everything still works.

1. Paste a notice

Press Ctrl+Enter (Cmd+Enter on a Mac) to decode. On a phone, install the app and share any notice to it straight from WhatsApp.

Photos and PDFs are read on this device (nothing is uploaded). The first photo downloads the reader once (about 10 MB); after that it works offline. Drop a file on the box, or paste a screenshot.

      Important notes

        5. Add to calendar and share

        Class representatives: share once, the whole class adds it in one tap. The events travel inside the link; nothing is stored on a server.

        6. Class calendar

        Class representatives publish once; the class subscribes once to a calendar that updates itself when a notice changes. Term-wide clashes are checked on every publish.
